Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy applies to the iPhone game Starship: Eclipse Assault, provided by independent developer Andrei Bayarka under the Hulio Studio name. Contact: huliostudiosupport@gmail.com.

In brief

The game does not collect or transmit your personal data to us. There are no user accounts, advertising networks, tracking tools or third-party analytics SDKs. Gameplay does not require a connection to our servers.

Information stored on your device

The game stores campaign progress, unlocked levels, stars, local scores, earned currency, ship upgrades, onboarding status and preferences using local iOS storage (UserDefaults). These records are used to restore your game and settings. We do not receive them. The game does not read contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location or advertising identifiers.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you reset progress or remove the app. Use Settings → Reset Progress inside the game to erase its saved progress and preferences. Deleting the app also removes its local app data. iOS device backups may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings; restoring a backup may restore saved data. We cannot access, recover or remotely delete your device's saved game.

Tracking, advertising and sharing

We do not track players across apps or websites, sell personal information, or share gameplay records with advertising or analytics providers. No account or in-app purchase is required in this version.

If you contact support

If you email us voluntarily, we receive your email address, the message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information only to answer your request, investigate the issue and maintain necessary support records. Our email provider, Google, processes these messages under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not send passwords or payment details. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us; we will delete it when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

External links and this website

Opening the privacy or support page launches an external browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and access logs when you visit, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no analytics, advertising, cookies, web forms or third-party scripts to this website. Email links open your chosen email application.

Apple services

The App Store and iOS may process purchase, device or diagnostic information independently under Apple's policies and your device settings. We have not added a crash-reporting or analytics service to the game. Any diagnostics Apple makes available to developers are governed by Apple's services and your sharing choices.

Children

The game does not collect personal information through gameplay, including from children. If you believe a child sent personal information in a support message, contact us to request its removal.

Your questions and choices

You control local game records through Reset Progress and iOS app removal. For questions about privacy, or access to or deletion of information you voluntarily emailed us, contact huliostudiosupport@gmail.com.

Changes

If our practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app and its App Store privacy information as appropriate.
