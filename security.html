Responsible reporting

Report a concern privately.

If you find a potential security or privacy issue in Starship: Eclipse Assault or this support website, contact Hulio Studio directly.

Email a security report

huliostudiosupport@gmail.com

What to include

Describe the affected version, the issue, its potential impact and minimal steps to reproduce it. Please do not include another person's data, passwords, tokens or payment information. Do not publish sensitive details in a public GitHub issue.

Scope

The current App Store release of the game and this support website are in scope. We do not operate a player account system or gameplay backend. GitHub, Apple and Google services are operated by their respective providers; issues in those services should be reported to them.

Safe testing

Test only on your own devices and data. Do not disrupt services or access information that is not yours. This page does not authorize testing third-party systems and does not establish a paid bug-bounty program.

How we respond

We review reports and may request further details. Please allow time for investigation before publishing technical details. We cannot promise a fixed response or remediation deadline.
