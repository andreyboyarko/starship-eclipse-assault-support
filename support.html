Reporting a problem

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, level number and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots can help, but remove notifications and personal information first. Do not send passwords or payment details.

How do I steer?

Touch the playfield and drag. The ship follows relative finger movement, so you do not need to cover the ship with your finger. Tap Pause to stop a mission.

How do upgrades work?

Earn currency during missions and spend it in Upgrades. Damage, Fire Rate, Shield Strength and Energy Capacity improve your ship. Its appearance changes at six and thirteen total upgrade levels and is applied when you start a mission.

Where is my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. The game does not provide an account or cross-device cloud sync. Reinstalling the app can remove your progress. Device backup and restore behavior depends on your iOS settings.

How do I erase my data?

Open Settings → Reset Progress and confirm. This resets local game records and preferences. This action cannot be undone inside the game.

Sound and accessibility

Gameplay effects can be disabled in Settings. Menus and the introduction remain silent. The game also provides haptics, screen-shake and flash settings and respects Reduce Motion for the launch animation.
